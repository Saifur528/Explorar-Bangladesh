About Chattogram Division

Chattogram Division is the largest division of Bangladesh and is famous for its natural beauty, hills, sea beaches, rivers and the country's busiest seaport.

Visitors can explore Patenga Sea Beach, the Karnaphuli River, Bandarban, Rangamati, Kaptai Lake and enjoy unforgettable scenic landscapes throughout the region.

Travel Tips

Useful tips before visiting Chattogram Division.

Best Time

Visit between October and March for pleasant weather and outdoor activities.

Budget

Carry cash for local markets, though cards and mobile banking are widely accepted.

Navigation

Use Google Maps, ride-sharing apps or local transport to explore the city and nearby hills.

Safety

Stay alert in crowded areas and follow local guidelines when visiting hills and beaches.
