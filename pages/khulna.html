About Khulna Division

Khulna Division is one of the most beautiful regions of Bangladesh and is famous for the Sundarbans, rivers, historical heritage and coastal landscapes.

Visitors can explore the Sundarbans, Sixty Dome Mosque, Kuakata, Shat Gombuj Mosque and many other remarkable natural and historical attractions throughout the region.

Top Tourist Attractions

Discover the most popular tourist attractions in Khulna Division.

Sundarbans

The world's largest mangrove forest, famous for its wildlife, rivers and beautiful natural landscapes.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Sixty Dome Mosque

A historic mosque in Bagerhat and one of Bangladesh's most remarkable UNESCO World Heritage sites.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Kuakata

A beautiful coastal destination famous for spectacular sunrise and sunset views over the Bay of Bengal.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Shat Gombuj Mosque

A magnificent historic mosque in Bagerhat known for its unique architecture and numerous domes.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Andhar Manik

A historic architectural attraction surrounded by greenery, showcasing the rich heritage of the Khulna region.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Dublar Char

A beautiful coastal island in the Sundarbans known for fishing, beaches and peaceful natural surroundings.

Famous Foods of Khulna

Taste the most delicious and traditional foods of Khulna Division.

Chingri Malai Curry

Delicious prawns cooked in creamy coconut milk with aromatic spices and a rich coastal flavor.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Chingri Bhorta

A spicy and flavorful mashed prawn dish prepared with chilies, onions and traditional Bengali spices.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Koi Macher Bhuna

Traditional climbing perch cooked with rich spices, onions and green chilies for a delicious taste.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Chitol Macher Muitha

Soft fish dumplings made from Chitol fish and served in a flavorful traditional curry.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Patishapta Pitha

Traditional Bengali rice crepes filled with sweet coconut and milk-based stuffing.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

A delicate traditional milk sweet made from fresh chhana and topped with nuts.

Transportation in Khulna

Explore the transportation options available across Khulna Division.

Khulna Railway

Comfortable railway services connect Khulna with Dhaka and other major cities of Bangladesh.

Bus

Affordable bus services connect Khulna with Dhaka and other major cities across Bangladesh.

CNG Auto

A convenient and popular local transport option for short-distance travel around Khulna.

River Boat

Traditional boats provide scenic transportation through the rivers and waterways of Khulna.

Launch

Passenger launches connect Khulna with various river routes and nearby coastal destinations.

Uber / Pathao

Ride-sharing services provide convenient transportation around Khulna city.

Travel Tips

Useful tips before visiting Khulna Division.

Best Time

Visit between October and March for pleasant weather and comfortable outdoor activities.

Budget

Carry cash for local markets, transport and rural areas, while mobile banking is also widely available.

Navigation

Use Google Maps, local transport and ride-sharing services to explore Khulna city and nearby attractions.

Safety

Stay alert in crowded areas and follow local guidelines when visiting the Sundarbans and coastal areas.
