About Rajshahi Division

Rajshahi Division is one of the most historically important and culturally rich regions of Bangladesh. It is well known for its archaeological heritage, beautiful landscapes, mangoes and famous silk.

Visitors can explore ancient archaeological sites, historic mosques, temples, museums, rivers and beautiful natural attractions throughout the region.

Travel Tips

Useful tips before visiting Rajshahi Division.

Best Time

Visit between October and March for pleasant weather and comfortable sightseeing and outdoor activities.

Budget

Carry some cash for local transport, markets and rural areas, while mobile banking is also widely available.

Navigation

Use Google Maps, local transport and easy bikes to explore Rajshahi city and nearby historical attractions.

Safety

Stay alert in crowded areas and follow local guidelines when visiting historical sites and riverside areas.
