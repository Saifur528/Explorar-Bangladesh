About Dhaka Division

Dhaka Division is the heart of Bangladesh. It is home to the country's capital city and serves as the center of politics, business, education and culture.

Visitors can enjoy historical landmarks like Ahsan Manzil, Lalbagh Fort, National Parliament, Hatirjheel, Sonargaon, and experience one of South Asia's fastest growing cities.

Travel Tips

Useful tips before visiting Dhaka Division.

Best Time

Visit between November and February for pleasant weather.

Budget

Carry enough cash or use Mobile Banking and ATM services.

Navigation

Use Google Maps or ride-sharing apps for easy travel.

Safety

Keep your valuables secure and avoid crowded areas at night.
