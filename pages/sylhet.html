About Sylhet Division

Sylhet Division is one of Bangladesh's most beautiful regions, renowned for its lush tea gardens, green hills, rivers and rich cultural heritage.

Visitors can explore Ratargul Swamp Forest, Jaflong, Bichanakandi, Lalakhal, Hazrat Shah Jalal (R.) Shrine and many other breathtaking destinations.

Transportation in Sylhet

Explore the transportation options available across Sylhet Division.

Sylhet's main international airport connecting the region with major destinations.

Bus

Comfortable bus services connect Sylhet with Dhaka and other major cities.

CNG Auto

One of the most common and convenient local transport options in Sylhet.

Sylhet Railway

Comfortable railway services connect Sylhet with Dhaka and other parts of Bangladesh.

Uber / Pathao

Ride-sharing services provide convenient transportation around Sylhet city.

Travel Tips

Useful tips before visiting Sylhet Division.

Best Time

Visit between October and March for pleasant weather and outdoor activities.

Budget

Carry cash for local transport, food, and small purchases in rural areas.

Navigation

Use Google Maps and local transport to explore tea gardens, rivers, and nearby attractions.

Safety

Stay careful near rivers, waterfalls, and hilly areas, especially during rainy weather.
